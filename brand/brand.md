# Merkster brand kit

- **Name:** Merkster (German *merken* = to remember / commit to memory)
- **Mark:** an M that is also a check mark (“got it”) plus a coral spark. Ink tile, acid-lime stroke.
- **Palette**
  - Ink `#0d0f1e` / Ink-2 `#1c2140`
  - Acid lime `#c6f432` / Lime-2 `#a6e00f`
  - Coral `#ff5a36`
  - Blue `#3d5afe` · Teal `#00c2a8` · Sun `#ffd23f`
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans (variable, 200–800)
- **Do not:** use a Merck-like logotype, serifs, or a medical cross; Merkster is a school-prep app for kids.
- Files: `logo.svg`, `logo-512.png`, `logo-192.png`, `favicon.svg`. The living brand sheet (`brand-sheet.png`) is produced by `tests/e2e/brand.spec.ts` from real app screenshots.
